# 75-league — monthly league by season trophies + watch live (2026-10-10)
- `prog.season={key:'YYYY-MM', tr, peak, floor}` (month from `dayKey()`); per real match: win 4/6/8/10/12/15 by level, online 10, classic +2; draw +2; loss −4 clamped at `floor`, no loss in Bronze. Leagues `ECON.league`: ברונזה 0 · כסף 20 · זהב 50 · פלטינה 100 · יהלום 200 · אלוף 400; crossing a threshold sets `floor`.
- Rollover (`leagueCheckRollover`, at load + on home): the prize of the finished season's peak league goes into `prog.seasonPrize` → modal `#league-prize-modal` ("קבל!": Silver 100🪙 · Gold 200🪙+1🔑 · Platinum 300🪙+10💎 · Diamond 500🪙+25💎 · Champion 800🪙+50💎, plus a silver chest), then tr=min(100,floor(peak/2)), floor = tr≥20 ? 20 : 0.
- Heartbeat adds `str, sk, lg`. Leaderboard: tab `#fr-tab-league` "🏅 ליגה החודש" (re-rendered from `users` by season trophies), and a "👀 צפה" button on every online+in-match row (both views) → `watchFriend(name)`.
- Home: `#home-league` pill ("🏅 <short league> · N", shares a row with the club pill) → `#league-modal`. End card: "🏅 +N לעונה".
- Public: `leagueMonthKey seasonState seasonPoints seasonApply leagueIndex leagueTier leagueName leagueShort leagueIcon leagueBadge leagueDaysLeft leagueCheckRollover leagueShowPrize leagueClaimPrize openLeagueModal closeLeagueModal leagueRefreshPill leagueRowsFromUsers leagueTopHTML leagueRenderTop leagueAddWatchButtons`.
